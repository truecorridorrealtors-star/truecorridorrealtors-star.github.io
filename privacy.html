Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

True Corridor Realtors ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we receive your name, phone number, email address (if given), the type of property you're interested in, your budget range and any message you write.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and share property options you've asked about. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Both may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we're advising you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, unless you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing truecorridorrealtors@gmail.com.
